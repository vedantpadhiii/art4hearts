# Chapter Good Standing

Every chapter leader signs the **Chapter Guidelines Agreement** when the chapter is founded. That
agreement, together with the onboarding meeting, defines what a chapter is expected to do. Staying
in good standing means following it.

## The Chapter Guidelines Agreement

This is the core requirement. All chapter leaders must read and sign it, and it is re-submitted
periodically — Art4Hearts sends an **[ACTION REQUIRED]** email with a deadline when a new round
opens. Chapters that miss it are asked to submit late rather than being dropped immediately, but
submitting on time is the expectation.

New chapter leaders also attend an onboarding meeting. If you miss it, Art4Hearts shares a
recording rather than requiring you to wait for the next session.

## Approved chapter activities

Art4Hearts chapters make and donate **handmade bracelets** and **art therapy kits**. Those are the
approved service activities.

**Other charitable activities, however well-intentioned, are not approved chapter activities** and
should not be run or posted as Art4Hearts events. Two real examples that drew warnings: **card-making
events** and **caroling** at a senior facility. Both are kind things to do — they are simply not
what an Art4Hearts chapter is for, and running them under the Art4Hearts name misrepresents what
the nonprofit does.

If you want to do something outside the usual two, ask first. Email art4heartsorg@gmail.com and
describe what you have in mind.

## What good standing looks like

- **Signed, current Chapter Guidelines Agreement**
- **Approved activities only** — bracelets and art therapy kits
- **Content guidelines followed** — no seasonal, holiday-themed, or religiously affiliated items.
  See [[Kit and Bracelet Content Guidelines]]
- **Donations routed correctly** — bracelets to a local hospital, senior center, or shelter; art
  therapy kits may be mailed to the national address. See [[Donating and Volunteer Hours]]
- **Items and hours logged** through the submission forms, so chapter activity is tracked
- **Active leadership and Instagram** — a working leadership team and a chapter account following
  the shared format. Posts don't need pre-approval, but must follow the Instagram guidelines
- **Responsible outreach** — confirm with a hospital or facility before assembling items for them,
  since some won't accept handmade donations

## If a chapter falls out of compliance

Art4Hearts contacts the chapter directly, asks for the issue to be corrected — for example removing
social media posts showing an unapproved activity — and treats it as an official warning. Repeated
or continued non-compliance can result in **chapter closure**.

## Questions

For the specific criteria tied to renewal, funding, or recognition, email art4heartsorg@gmail.com
and the Chapter Directors can give you the exact requirements.

# Contacting Art4Hearts

Art4Hearts is run by a team of student leaders. All enquiries go to one general inbox and are
routed internally to the right person.

## How to get in touch

**Email: art4heartsorg@gmail.com**

That address handles everything — chapters, partnerships, press, volunteering, and website issues.
Describe your situation in the email and the team routes it internally to the right person.

There is no subject-line convention. Don't tell people to put a particular word in the subject
line.

You can also reach Art4Hearts on Instagram at **@art4heartsorg**, or through the website at
<https://art4heartsinternational.org>.

## Financial donations

Art4Hearts is **not accepting monetary donations yet** — a giving path is coming soon. To help now,
volunteer or start a chapter.

## Note on names

Art4Hearts' leaders are students. The assistant does not give out individual names or personal
contact details, and routes people to the general inbox instead. This is deliberate — see
`GUARDRAILS.md`.

# Donating and Volunteer Hours

Art4Hearts accepts handmade bracelets and art therapy kits from volunteers anywhere.

## Getting service hours

Service hours at Art4Hearts are always verified — there is no informal or unverified alternative.
The process is two forms:

1. **Volunteer Registration Form** — registers you as an Art4Hearts volunteer.
2. **Service Hours Request Form** — logs the items you made and the hours you spent. There is a
   dedicated version for bracelets.

Both are required. Hours are self-reported: track the time you spend making your items and enter it
on the form. Items donated without a logged form generally cannot be credited afterward, so fill
the form out.

## Donating without claiming hours

If you don't want service hours, you can simply donate. Fill out the **Bracelet Donation Form** and
that's the whole process — no registration needed.

## If you are volunteering as a group or organization

Groups don't have to file individually. Art4Hearts shares a spreadsheet template, and the group
submits one record containing:

- Volunteer name
- Number of hours volunteered
- Media proof of the completed items
- Proof of donation — a mailing receipt, or evidence of delivery to a charitable organization
- Preferred certification format (certificate, signed e-form, etc.)

## Where donations go

**The rule depends on what you made.**

| Item | Mail to the P.O. box? | Donate locally? |
|---|---|---|
| **Bracelets** | **No** — not accepted since 2026-07-31 | Yes |
| **Art therapy kits** | Yes | Yes |

**Bracelets must be donated to a charitable organization near you** — a hospital, senior center, or
shelter. As of **2026-07-31** Art4Hearts no longer accepts bracelets at its P.O. box. Two reasons:
it reduces the org's environmental footprint, and it pushes support into volunteers' own
communities for wider impact. This applies to chapters and individual volunteers alike.

**You still get full service hours for bracelets donated locally.** Nothing about hour verification
changed — only the destination.

**Art therapy kits can still be mailed.** Going forward the P.O. box is reserved exclusively for
kits. Send them to:

```
Art4Hearts
14435 Big Basin Way, Suite C, PMB 175
Saratoga, CA 95070
```

**Do not mail bracelets to this address.**

Volunteers pay for their own supplies and postage. Art4Hearts hopes to offer financial aid once
donation funds allow.

## If you can't find anywhere local to donate

Register as a volunteer first — Art4Hearts provides outreach email templates and resources to help
you approach hospitals, senior centers, and shelters in your area. Keep trying different types of
organization: hospitals in particular often decline homemade items, while a senior center or
shelter nearby may accept them gladly.

If you've genuinely exhausted your local options, **email art4heartsorg@gmail.com, describe your
situation, and the team can help**. They may be able to point you toward a nearby Art4Hearts
chapter using the chapter map at <https://art4heartsinternational.org/about/chapters>, so you can
donate through an established chapter instead.

# Kit and Bracelet Content Guidelines

Art4Hearts kits go to hospitals, senior centers, and shelters serving people of many backgrounds
and beliefs. Two content restrictions follow from that.

## Excluded from kits

- **Seasonal or holiday-themed items** — Christmas, Halloween, and similar
- **Items with religious affiliations**

This was stated directly to a materials supplier in 2026-03 as a standing requirement, not a
one-off request.

## Why

Kits are distributed to recipients whose holidays and beliefs Art4Hearts doesn't know in advance.
Neutral materials mean any kit can go to any recipient without causing discomfort.

# Starting a Chapter

Students who want to bring Art4Hearts to their school or community found a chapter. The national
organization provides a shared resource set; the chapter runs its own local operations.

## How to start one

**Apply to be a chapter lead on the Art4Hearts website** — <https://art4heartsinternational.org>.
That application is the entry point; everything below follows once you're approved.

## What a new chapter sets up

**Leadership.** A founder, and typically a president, vice president, secretary, and treasurer.
School-based chapters usually also have a faculty advisor.

**School club approval.** Chapters at a school go through their school's normal club application
process. Art4Hearts provides example application statements and will optionally review a draft
application before submission.

**A chapter email address.** Most chapters use a dedicated address rather than a personal one.

**Social media.** Chapters run their own Instagram account, following a shared format — a chapter
profile picture, and a bio identifying the chapter's city and noting it is a chapter of
@art4heartsorg.

## What the national organization provides

- A chapter resources folder with templates and guides
- Bracelet-making and art-therapy-kit-making presentations
- Submission forms for logging completed bracelets and kits
- An outreach organization tracker to copy
- Outreach email templates for approaching partner organizations
- A nonprofit operating budget template
- Guidance and resources for grant applications

## Running outreach

Chapters contact local organizations directly — hospitals, retirement and senior living centers,
homeless shelters. Chapters are advised that some hospitals will not accept homemade items and
should confirm first.

# Volunteering Basics

The most common questions people ask before signing up.

## Who can volunteer

**Anyone, of any age, anywhere in the world.** Art4Hearts is a global organization — you do not
need to be in the United States, and you do not need to be in high school.

## How to start

Fill out the **Volunteer Registration Form**. You should receive a confirmation email **within 24
hours**.

If nothing arrives: check that the email address you entered is correct and that your inbox isn't
full, then resubmit the form or email [[Contacting Art4Hearts|art4heartsorg@gmail.com]].

## Costs

Volunteers pay for their own supplies and postage. Art4Hearts hopes to offer financial aid in
future once donation funds allow.

## Finding somewhere to donate

Once you register, Art4Hearts provides outreach email templates and resources so you can approach
hospitals, senior centers, and shelters in your own area independently.

## Service hours

All Art4Hearts service hours are verified — register first, then log your hours on the Service Hours
Request Form. Hours are self-reported: track the time you spend making your items. See
[[Donating and Volunteer Hours]] for the full process.

# Art4Hearts Overview

Art4Hearts is a student-run nonprofit that spreads kindness through art. Volunteers make **art
therapy kits** and **handmade bracelets** and donate them to hospitals, senior living and retirement
centers, homeless shelters, and similar community organizations. The work is carried out by
student-led chapters, most of them clubs based at a high school.

The organization's public tagline is **"Spreading joy and hope through art, one bracelet at a
time."** It is a registered nonprofit, **EIN 33-2190440**, on the web at
**art4heartsinternational.org** and on Instagram at **@art4heartsorg**.

Volunteering is open to anyone, of any age, anywhere in the world.

## Founding

Art4Hearts was **founded in 2024** by co-founders **Grace Lin** and **Ruiyan Zhu**. It has grown to
over 150 chapters worldwide in roughly two years.

## Impact

- **5,500+ volunteers**
- **10,000+ bracelets and art therapy kits donated**
- **150+ chapters** worldwide

## Financial donations

Art4Hearts is **not currently accepting monetary donations** — a giving path is planned but not
live yet. People who want to help right now should volunteer: make bracelets or kits, or start a
chapter. See [[Volunteering Basics]] and [[Starting a Chapter]].

## What Art4Hearts does

- **[[Art Therapy Kits]]** — assembled kits (watercolor, crochet, and other formats depending on
  what the receiving organization prefers) donated to community partners.
- **[[Bracelets]]** — volunteer-made bracelets given as small gestures of warmth and connection.

Both programs run through chapters rather than centrally.

## Chapters

Art4Hearts is a chapter network: students found a chapter at their school or in their community,
register it with the national organization, and run local outreach and making sessions using shared
templates and resources. See [[Chapter Network]] for where chapters exist and
[[Starting a Chapter]] for how the process works.

As of the most recent roster, the network spans roughly 55 chapters across the United States and
seven other countries.

## How to get involved

- **Start a chapter** at your school or in your community — see [[Starting a Chapter]].
- **Follow on Instagram** at @art4heartsorg for current activity.
- **Partner with a chapter** if you represent a hospital, senior center, shelter, or similar
  organization that could use kits or bracelets.

# Art Therapy Kits

Art therapy kits are assembled by chapter volunteers and donated to community organizations. The
kits are tailored to the recipient — Art4Hearts asks partner organizations what they'd actually
use rather than sending a fixed product. Watercolor kits and crochet kits are both common formats.

## Who receives them

Chapters approach hospitals, senior living and retirement centers, and homeless shelters. Some
hospitals do not accept homemade items, so chapters are advised to confirm before assembling.

## How chapters run it

Chapters have a shared kit-making presentation and a submission form for logging completed kits.
Outreach to a prospective partner starts with a shared email template that chapters adapt.

# Bracelets

Chapter volunteers make bracelets by hand and donate them to community organizations. Art4Hearts
describes them as "a small but warm way to brighten their day" — the intent is a gesture of joy and
connection rather than a material need.

Bracelets are Art4Hearts' highest-volume program — the majority of volunteer activity and donations
flow through them.

## Who receives them

The same partner categories as [[Art Therapy Kits]]: hospitals, senior centers, retirement
communities, homeless shelters.

## How chapters run it

Chapters work from a shared bracelet-making presentation and log completed bracelets through a
submission form. Outreach uses a dedicated email template.

## Donating them

See [[Donating and Volunteer Hours]] for the forms. **Bracelets must be donated to a local
organization** — a hospital, senior center, or shelter. Art4Hearts stopped accepting bracelets at
its P.O. box on **2026-07-31** (kits are still accepted there). Local donation earns full service
hours.

## Accepted bracelet types

Charm, beaded, crocheted, braided, and friendship/string bracelets. Jewelry glue and metal clasps
are fine.

**Rainbow loom bracelets are not accepted.**

## Making them

1. Fill out the Volunteer Registration Form
2. Review the Bracelets Service Hours Request Form so you know how to track time
3. Make bracelets
4. Log your hours on the Bracelets Service Hours Request Form

Hours are self-reported — you track the time you spend making your creations.

## Documented donations

A shipment of 300+ bracelets went to [[Partner Organizations|Hopecam]] under a prepaid label
(2025-09 to 2026-07). Hopecam set no minimum or maximum quantity.
